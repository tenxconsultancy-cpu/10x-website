How it Works

The 10X Path

A rigorous, phase-gated methodology designed to identify, build, and scale AI utilities that drive actual business growth.

Phase 1

The 10X Audit (The Blueprint)

Deep-dive discovery through stakeholder interviews and workflow analysis, opportunity mapping, and a comprehensive strategic roadmap including cost-benefit analysis and implementation timelines.

Phase 2

Agile Implementation

Architecture design and data grounding to build custom AI workflow infrastructure, integrating seamlessly with your existing ERP/BIM software and ensuring AI operates on your proprietary data securely.

Phase 3

Rigorous Testing & QA

Stress testing through edge-case scenarios to ensure 100% reliability in high-stakes environments, combined with security validation to meet international privacy and corporate standards.

Deployment & Knowledge Transfer

Live integration into daily operations with zero downtime, complemented by custom playbooks and hands-on training for high adoption rates across your team.

Phase 5

Evaluation & Continuous Improvement

Impact validation against pre-audit benchmarks (time saved, costs reduced), followed by an optimization loop for fine-tuning AI workflows based on real-world feedback to ensure continuous evolution.
